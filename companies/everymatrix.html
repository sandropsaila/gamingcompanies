EveryMatrix

B2B iGaming software provider founded and headquartered in Malta, supplying casino, sportsbook, payments and affiliate/agent-management platforms to operators and state lotteries. EveryMatrix does not operate any B2C gambling business itself.

Relationship with Malta

  • EveryMatrix was founded in Malta in 2008 and remains headquartered in Sliema, Malta — making Malta its home jurisdiction rather than simply an operational hub.
  • The company holds a Malta Gaming Authority B2B licence (MGA/B2B/201/2011, per its own disclosure) covering fixed-odds betting, including live betting, since securing it in 2011.
  • EveryMatrix is a long-standing member of Malta's B2B iGaming supplier cluster.
