EveryMatrix

B2B iGaming software provider founded and headquartered in Malta, supplying casino, sportsbook and payments platforms; holds no B2C business of its own.

Overview

Founded
2008, Malta — co-founders Ebbe Groes & Stian Hornsletten
Operational HQ
Malta, with 16 offices across Europe, Asia and the US
Group registration
EveryMatrix Ltd, Malta
Sector
B2B iGaming software: casino platform, sportsbook (OddsMatrix), payments, PAM/wallet
Employees
1,500+ across 16 offices
Ownership
Privately held; founder-led, with Ebbe Groes and (from January 2026) his brother Jonas Groes as Co-CEOs.

Brands

OddsMatrix

Sportsbook platform

Sports-betting risk/trading engine; 2024 segment revenue €51.0m (+75% YoY), betting volumes up 14x in five years.

Spearhead Studios / Armadillo Studios / Fantasma Games

In-house game studios

Proprietary casino content studios (launched 2019, 2021; Fantasma acquired 2024), moving EveryMatrix beyond pure platform provision.

Verticals

B2B casino platform B2B sportsbook (OddsMatrix) Payments / PAM & wallet Proprietary game content

Markets

Global — B2B supplier, incl. state lotteries (e.g. Finland's Veikkaus, National Lottery of Malta)

As a B2B supplier, EveryMatrix's footprint is defined by client operator/lottery relationships rather than its own consumer gaming licences.

Relationship with Malta Founded & HQ: Malta

  • EveryMatrix was founded in Malta in 2008 and remains headquartered there — one of relatively few large iGaming suppliers actually founded (not just later registered) in the jurisdiction.
  • EveryMatrix's B2B Maltese licence (referenced elsewhere in this dossier as MGA/B2B/201/2011) is used to supply platform and content services to numerous other MGA-licensed operators, including several other files in this collection.
