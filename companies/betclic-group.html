Betclic Group

French sports betting, poker and casino operator headquartered in Bordeaux, with its international operations based in Malta since 2007. Now part of Banijay Gaming alongside Tipico.

Overview

Founded
2005, by Eric Moncada & Nicolas Béraud
Operational HQ
Bordeaux, France (French operations); Malta (international operations, since 2007)
Group registration
Betclic Group; international entities registered in Malta
Sector
Sports betting, poker, online casino
Employees
Not independently confirmed (mid-size, multi-country: Bordeaux, Malta, Portugal, Italy, Poland, Ivory Coast offices)
Ownership
Subsidiary of Banijay Gaming (the sports-betting/online-gaming arm of Banijay Group, listed in Amsterdam). Banijay struck a deal with CVC Capital Partners in Oct 2025 to acquire a controlling 65% of Tipico Group, merging Tipico and Betclic under Banijay Gaming.

Betclic

Market leader positioning in French sports betting historically (35-40% share cited circa 2010-13); merged with Everest Poker in 2016.
