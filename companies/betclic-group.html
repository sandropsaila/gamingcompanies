Betclic Group

French-founded online sports betting, casino and poker operator. French operations are headquartered in Bordeaux; its Malta office, opened in 2007, houses trading, customer relations and compliance functions for its international business.

Relationship with Malta

  • Betclic Group opened its first office in Malta in 2007, the first jurisdiction to grant online gaming operator licences, and continues to operate from there today.
  • Malta hosts Betclic's sports trading services, customer relations, and fraud-prevention/anti-money-laundering operational functions, alongside responsible-gambling monitoring teams.
  • Malta sits alongside Bordeaux, Lisbon and other Betclic Group hubs as part of its international operating footprint.
