Relationship with Malta

  • Novibet's group headquarters is stated by the company itself to be in Malta, with a technology hub in Greece and additional staff in the Isle of Man and Italy.
  • The company is licensed and regulated in Malta by the MGA alongside its other jurisdictional licences (HGC in Greece, ADM in Italy, Irish Revenue Commissioners).
  • Malta hosts core compliance and regulatory functions for the group's pan-European and Latin American expansion.
