MGA Authorisation

NoteSportradar Malta Limited (Malta company C 90853, incorporated 27 Feb 2019, Ta' Xbiex) does not hold a standard MGA B2B/B2C licence on the Dynamic Seal system used elsewhere in this dossier. The reference it appears under on other operators' licences (RN/178/2020) is a Recognition Notice — a distinct, lighter-touch MGA mechanism under which an authorisation already held in another EU/EEA state (or an equivalent jurisdiction) is recognised as having the same effect as a Maltese authorisation. Recognition Notices sit on a separate register (the MGA's Recognition Notice Register) from the Dynamic Seal of Authorisation used for full licensees, which is why the standard per-entity lookup used for the other files in this dossier does not apply here. This should be verified directly on the Recognition Notice Register before being relied upon.
