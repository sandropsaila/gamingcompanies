Sportradar

Nasdaq-listed (SRAD) sports data and technology company — a B2B supplier of odds, streaming and integrity services to sportsbooks, leagues and media, not a consumer-facing betting operator. Headquartered in St. Gallen, Switzerland, with a Maltese subsidiary, Sportradar Malta Ltd.

Sportradar does not itself take consumer bets; it licenses data and technology to sportsbooks and sports bodies globally, with 29+ offices in 20 countries as of 2024, including a Maltese subsidiary used for the company's European operations.

MGA Licences

Not applicable — see note.

NoteSportradar is a B2B data and technology provider, not a consumer-facing gaming operator, so it does not appear on the MGA's public licensee register in the way a B2C operator would. Its Maltese entity, Sportradar Malta Ltd, is confirmed to exist via Swiss commercial-register cross-references, but no MGA gaming licence tied to it was identified.

Relationship with Malta

  • Sportradar Malta Ltd exists as a registered subsidiary of the Sportradar group, cross-referenced in Swiss commercial-register filings for the Swiss parent entity.
  • As a B2B data/technology supplier rather than a consumer-facing gambling operator, Sportradar's Maltese presence is understood to primarily support European commercial and operational functions rather than an MGA consumer gaming licence.
  • Malta's role in Sportradar's structure could not be further detailed from public sources in this search — confirm directly with the company for the specific function of the Maltese entity.
