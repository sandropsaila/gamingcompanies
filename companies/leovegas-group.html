LeoVegas Group

Swedish mobile-first casino and sports betting group, a subsidiary of MGM Resorts International since 2022. Group brand management is based in Stockholm, with its main operations hub in Malta.

Relationship with Malta

  • LeoVegas Gaming Ltd is the group's Maltese entity and is described by the company as its principal operations hub, holding key operating licences for its casino and sportsbook services.
  • Malta serves as LeoVegas Group's primary licensing and compliance base even though brand and group management sits in Stockholm.
  • The MGA's public licensee register is the authoritative source to confirm the entity's current licence status.

Relationship with Deloitte

  • No publicly disclosed audit, assurance or advisory engagement between Deloitte and LeoVegas Group (or MGM Resorts) was identified in this search.
  • As an MGA-licensed operator, LeoVegas falls within the general population covered by Deloitte Malta's sector-wide iGaming research rather than being evidenced as a client.
  • Any actual engagement should be confirmed against Deloitte's internal client and independence records.
