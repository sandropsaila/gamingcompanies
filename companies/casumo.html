Casumo

Privately held, Malta-headquartered online casino and sportsbook group operating Casumo, Dunder, Kazoom and Casino Secret, with five MGA B2C licensees.

Brands

Mobile-first casino and sportsbook brand launched in 2012, operated through Casumo Services Limited and sister licensees.

Dunder

Second group casino brand, named alongside Casumo in the group's own description of its brand portfolio.

Casino Secret

Acquired casino brand

Relationship with Malta Malta HQ & MGA licensed

  • Casumo is headquartered in Malta (Unicorn Centre, Swieqi) and its group holding company is Maltese.
  • Five group entities hold MGA B2C Gaming Service Licences, all confirmed live on the MGA Dynamic Seal.
  • Malta's FIAU fined Casumo Services Limited €66,476 on 31 Aug 2023 for AML breaches (see Negative Media).
