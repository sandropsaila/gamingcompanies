MediaTroopers

Privately held paid-media performance marketing agency for regulated US and Canadian sports betting and casino operators, founded in Pennsylvania with a Malta operations hub.

Overview

Founded
2018, by Shmulik "Sam" Segal and Benjamin Truman
Operational HQ
King of Prussia, Pennsylvania, USA, with a Malta hiring/operations hub (Sliema / Ta' Xbiex)
Group registration
Not independently confirmed as a single group entity in this search; operates as MediaTroopers in the US and through a newly established local entity in Brazil (announced June 2026)
Sector
iGaming affiliate marketing — paid media / mobile acquisition, not SEO-led
Employees
Not independently confirmed
Ownership
Privately held; founder-led by CEO Shmulik Segal. Co-founder and COO Benjamin Truman departed the company in October 2023.

Brands

MediaTroopers

Single agency brand

Operates its own portfolio of acquisition sites and paid-media campaigns (Google, Meta and social channels) driving traffic to licensed US and Canadian sportsbook and casino operators, rather than trading under multiple consumer-facing affiliate brands.

Verticals

Sports betting affiliate marketing Online casino affiliate marketing Paid media / mobile acquisition (not SEO)

Markets

21+ licensed US states (incl. New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Indiana, Michigan, Arizona, Maine) Ontario, Canada Washington DC Puerto Rico Brazil (new local entity announced June 2026)

MediaTroopers is licensed as an affiliate directly with individual US state gaming regulators (not the MGA) and also operates in states where no affiliate-level licence is required, such as Connecticut, Illinois, New York, Ohio and Nevada.

MGA Authorisation

Not applicable. MediaTroopers' business is licensed directly by individual US state gaming regulators (e.g. the Maine Gambling Control Unit) and, for its casino/sportsbook clients, ultimately governed by those states' regulatory frameworks — not the Malta Gaming Authority. A search of the MGA Dynamic Seal and Recognition Notice Register for MediaTroopers returned no matching licensee, consistent with the company having no Maltese gaming authorisation. Its Malta office is a hiring/operations hub, not a licensed gaming entity.

Segal previously led performance marketing at PokerStars' iBus Media and held product roles at Playtech and Aspire Global before co-founding MediaTroopers with Truman in 2018. Truman's departure in 2023 followed the company's expansion to over two dozen US states, DC and Ontario.

Relationship with Malta Operations hub, no gaming licence

  • MediaTroopers opened a Malta office (Sliema / Ta' Xbiex) to tap into the island's experienced iGaming talent pool, per its own CEO's account of the company's hiring strategy — a workforce/operations decision rather than a regulatory one.
  • No MGA gaming authorisation, Recognition Notice, or Malta-registered gaming entity for MediaTroopers was found in this search; its actual licensing relationships are with US state regulators.
