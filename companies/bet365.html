FILE 02 · OPERATOR PROFILE

bet365

Trading brand of the privately held Hillside Group. Parent entity Hillside (New Media Holdings) Limited is headquartered at Bet365 House, Stoke-on-Trent, England; Maltese operations run through Hillside (New Media Malta) plc.

Brands

bet365

Single unified consumer brand

Unlike multi-brand competitors, bet365 trades under one global consumer-facing brand across all product lines and territories, differentiated locally by licensed operating entity (e.g. Hillside (New Media Malta) plc, Hillside (Australia New Media) Pty Ltd).

bet365 holds licences in 9 EU member states plus Gibraltar and Australia per EGBA's own membership description, with additional regulated US states and Latin American markets added through FY2024/25. It has also historically served customers in unregulated markets and announced its exit from China in 2025 as part of a shift toward regulated markets.

Relationship with Malta MGA licensed

  • The Malta Gaming Authority granted a Class 2 Remote Gaming (Sports Betting) licence to Hillside (New Media Malta) Limited, confirmed on the MGA's own announcement and licensee register.
  • In conjunction with Maltese licensing, bet365 established a Maltese marketing company with premises in Sliema supporting marketing management and procurement functions for the group.
  • The Maltese licensed entity's registered office is Quantum House, Ta' Xbiex — placing bet365 alongside Kaizen Gaming and other major operators within Malta's MGA-licensed cluster.

Relationship with Deloitte

  • No publicly disclosed audit, assurance or advisory engagement between Deloitte and bet365 (or the wider Hillside Group) was identified in this search.
  • As an MGA licensee, bet365 sits within the population of operators covered by Deloitte Malta's general iGaming sector research and regulatory commentary, which is sector-wide rather than evidence of a client relationship.
