FILE 02 · OPERATOR PROFILE

bet365

Trading name of the privately held Hillside group of companies, founded and majority-owned by the Coates family. Stoke-on-Trent headquartered, licensed across the UK, Malta, Gibraltar, Australia and beyond.

Relationship with Malta MGA licensed

  • bet365 was awarded its original MGA Class II Remote Gaming licence in 2015 via Hillside (New Media Malta) Limited, alongside its longer-standing Gibraltar licences.
  • The active Maltese licence today sits with Hillside (Sports) ENC (MGA/CRP/531/2018), confirmed Licensed on the MGA's own Dynamic Seal.
  • Malta is one of several licensing hubs for bet365 (alongside the UK, Gibraltar and Australia) rather than its primary operating base, which remains Stoke-on-Trent.

Relationship with Deloitte

  • No publicly disclosed audit, assurance or advisory engagement between Deloitte and bet365/Hillside was identified in this search.
  • bet365, as a UK-headquartered MGA licensee, sits within the general population any Big Four firm's iGaming sector practice would track, but that is not evidence of a specific engagement.
