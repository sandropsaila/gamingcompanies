FILE 03 · OPERATOR PROFILE

Kindred Group

Owner of Unibet, 32Red and seven other consumer brands, plus the Relax Gaming B2B content business. Registered in Malta; became a wholly-owned subsidiary of France's La Française des Jeux (FDJ) following a 2024 acquisition.

Relationship with Malta

  • Kindred Group plc is registered in Malta under Company No. C 39017, with a registered office at Camilleri Preziosi, Valletta.
  • The group's operational base has historically been sited in Malta (Gzira/Sliema/Tigne Point offices), alongside its regulatory and compliance functions.
  • Kindred is a long-standing member of the Malta-based iGaming operator cluster alongside Betsson, LeoVegas and others.

Relationship with Deloitte

  • No publicly disclosed audit, assurance or advisory engagement between Deloitte and Kindred Group was identified in this search.
  • As a Malta-registered operator, Kindred sits within the general population of operators covered by Deloitte Malta's sector-wide iGaming research rather than being evidenced as a client.
  • Any actual engagement should be confirmed against Deloitte's internal client and independence records.
