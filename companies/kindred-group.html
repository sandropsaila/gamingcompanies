Kindred Group

Owner of Unibet and 32Red among nine consumer brands. Malta-registered plc (Kindred Group plc, C 39017), now a wholly-owned subsidiary of France's FDJ following a 2024/25 acquisition.

MGA Licence(s)

NoteConfirmed live via the MGA Dynamic Seal: Kindred IP Limited holds an active B2B Critical Supply Licence. Notably, Unibet's actual consumer-facing licensing does not run through this Maltese entity — per Kindred's own disclosures (corroborated on Wikipedia), Unibet is licensed for European customers via Platinum Gaming Limited under a Gibraltar licence (RGL 091 & RGL 092) and, for UK customers, a UK Gambling Commission licence. Kindred IP Limited's Maltese B2B licence appears to cover intra-group platform/IP supply rather than the primary consumer authorisation — worth flagging for due-diligence purposes, since Malta functions here as Kindred's corporate/holding jurisdiction rather than its primary consumer-licensing jurisdiction.

Relationship with Malta MGA registered

  • Kindred Group plc is incorporated and registered in Malta (C 39017), headquartered at Tigne Point, Sliema — not merely a licensing subsidiary but the group's holding and reporting entity.
  • Malta's Companies Act (specifically Article 151A) governed the company's statutory audit tender process, underlining how directly Maltese company law applies to the group's governance, not just its gaming licence.
  • Kindred is one of the longest-established MGA-linked groups, alongside Betsson, in the jurisdiction's cluster of Nasdaq-heritage Nordic operators based in Malta.

Relationship with Deloitte

  • No publicly disclosed Deloitte audit or advisory engagement was identified — Kindred's confirmed auditors are PwC (to FY2024) and KPMG (from FY2025), not Deloitte.
  • As with other files in this dossier, Kindred sits within the general population of Deloitte Malta's iGaming sector coverage without that implying any client relationship.
