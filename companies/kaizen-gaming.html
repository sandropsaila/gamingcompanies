Kaizen Gaming

Group parent: Kaizen Gaming International Limited (KGIL). Operating brands trade as Betano and Stoiximan. Athens is the operational headquarters; the group is registered and licensed out of Malta.

Ownership
Founder-controlled. Allwyn International (via Allwyn Investments Cyprus Ltd) holds a 36.75% minority interest in the international Betano business, acquired from OPAP in 2022. OPAP separately retains an 84.49% combined stake in the Greece/Cyprus-only Stoiximan business.

Brands

Betano

International premium brand

The group's flagship sports betting and gaming brand, active across Europe, the Americas and Africa. Widely sponsored in football (e.g. Aston Villa, FC Porto, FC Bayern Munich, River Plate, Flamengo) and an official FIFA World Cup 2026 tournament supporter for Europe and South America.

Stoiximan

Domestic brand — Greece & Cyprus

The original brand under which the group operates in its home markets, held separately from the internationally-focused Betano business for ownership purposes.

Relationship with Malta MGA licensed

  • Kaizen Gaming International Limited is registered in Malta (Unit 41/42, Pendergardens Business Centre, San Ġiljan) and holds a Malta Gaming Authority licence verified on the MGA's public licensee register.
  • The group's own corporate site footer states the company's HQ as Malta, even though day-to-day operations are run out of Athens — a common structure for MGA-licensed groups with an EU holding entity in Malta and an operational base elsewhere.
  • Malta sits alongside Betsson, Novibet and other MGA licensees as part of the jurisdiction's cluster of major iGaming groups.

Relationship with Deloitte

  • No publicly disclosed audit, assurance or advisory engagement between Deloitte and Kaizen Gaming (or Betano/Stoiximan) was identified in this search.
  • Kaizen Gaming, as an MGA-licensed operator, falls within the population of operators covered by Deloitte Malta's iGaming sector research (e.g. the Deloitte Gaming Pulse survey and industry benchmarking), which surveys the sector generally rather than implying a client relationship.
