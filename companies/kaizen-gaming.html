Kaizen Gaming

Group parent: Kaizen Gaming International Limited (KGIL). Operating brands trade as Betano and Stoiximan. Athens is the operational headquarters; the group is registered and licensed out of Malta.

Overview

Ownership
Founder-controlled. OPAP holds an 84.49% combined stake in the Greece/Cyprus-only Stoiximan Limited business. Allwyn International holds a 36.75% minority interest in the international Betano business (Kaizen Gaming International Limited), acquired from OPAP Investment Limited in 2022 for ≈€74.2m plus earn-outs.

Brands

Betano

International premium brand

Flagship sports betting and gaming brand active across Europe, the Americas and Africa. Official FIFA World Cup 2026™ supporter (Europe/South America), UEFA EURO 2024 betting sponsor, CONMEBOL partner.

Stoiximan

Domestic brand — Greece & Cyprus

The original brand, market leader in Greece and Cyprus with a reported 50%+ market share; held via the OPAP-majority-owned Stoiximan Limited, separate from the internationally-focused Betano business.

Relationship with Malta MGA licensed

  • Kaizen Gaming International Limited is registered in Malta (San Ġiljan) and holds two live MGA licences, verified directly via the Authority's Dynamic Seal of Authorisation.
  • Betano's operational hub is commonly cited as Sliema/San Ġiljan, Malta, even though day-to-day group operations run out of Athens — a common structure for MGA-licensed groups with an EU holding entity in Malta.
  • Malta sits alongside Betsson, Novibet and other MGA licensees as part of the jurisdiction's cluster of major iGaming groups.

Relationship with Deloitte

  • No publicly disclosed audit, assurance or advisory engagement between Deloitte and Kaizen Gaming (or Betano/Stoiximan) was identified in this search.
  • Kaizen Gaming, as an MGA-licensed operator, falls within the population of operators covered by Deloitte Malta's iGaming sector research (e.g. the Deloitte Gaming Pulse survey), which surveys the sector generally rather than implying a client relationship.
