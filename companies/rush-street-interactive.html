FILE 14 · OPERATOR PROFILE

Rush Street Interactive

NYSE-listed online casino and sports betting company (NYSE: RSI), headquartered in Chicago, operating exclusively in the United States and the rest of the Americas.

Overview

Founded
2012, Chicago, Illinois — co-founders Neil Bluhm, Greg Carlin & Richard Schwartz
Operational HQ
Chicago, Illinois, USA
Group registration
Rush Street Interactive, Inc., Delaware holding company; NYSE: RSI (listed via SPAC merger, December 2020)
Sector
Online casino & sports betting, plus a smaller social/free-to-play gaming business
Employees
≈ 757 (per third-party estimate, Mar 2026)
Ownership
Publicly listed; majority-controlled by its founders and by Rush Street Gaming, LLC (the privately held land-based casino company, also controlled by Neil Bluhm and Greg Carlin), which shares no executives with RSI beyond Bluhm as Executive Chairman of both.

Brands

BetRivers

Core US online casino & sportsbook brand

RSI's primary brand, active in fifteen-plus U.S. states, drawing on the Rivers-branded land-based casinos historically developed by Bluhm and Carlin.

PlaySugarHouse

Legacy US online casino brand

An earlier RSI online casino brand, retained in some markets alongside BetRivers.

RushBet

Latin America brand

RSI's brand for Latin American markets, including Colombia, where a 2025 tax-framework change temporarily reduced revenue by roughly $70 million before conditions improved.

Verticals

Online casino (iCasino) Online sports betting Retail sports betting Social / free-to-play gaming

Markets

United States (15+ states, incl. New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Indiana, Michigan, Delaware) Ontario, Canada Colombia Mexico Other Latin American markets

RSI operates exclusively in the Americas — it holds no European licences and is not part of the MGA-centred cluster covered by the rest of this dossier.

MGA Authorisation

Not applicable. Rush Street Interactive, Inc. (NYSE: RSI) holds no Malta Gaming Authority authorisation and has no Maltese operating entity — its licensed footprint is entirely within U.S. states, Ontario, and Latin American jurisdictions. Not to be confused with Rush Gaming Ltd (Malta company C 80159), an unrelated Malta-based white-label iGaming provider whose B2C licence (MGA/B2C/701/2019) the MGA suspended on 9 January 2024 and cancelled on 11 March 2024 for regulatory non-compliance. The shared "Rush" name is coincidental; no ownership or corporate link between the two entities was found in this search.

Schwartz succeeded Carlin as CEO in August 2021; Sauers was promoted to President in October 2025 while remaining CFO. Neil Bluhm is also Executive Chairman of the separately held Rush Street Gaming, LLC, the land-based casino company from which RSI takes its brand heritage.

Relationship with Malta No Malta presence

  • No evidence of an MGA authorisation, a Maltese operating entity, or any registered office in Malta for Rush Street Interactive, Inc. was found in this search — consistent with RSI's stated focus on the United States, Canada and Latin America only.
  • The only Malta-registered entity trading under a similar name, Rush Gaming Ltd (C 80159, St. Julian's, Malta — a white-label B2C iGaming provider), is unrelated: no shared ownership, directors, or corporate link to RSI was identified, and its MGA licence was suspended and then cancelled in 2024 for compliance failures unconnected to RSI.

Relationship with Deloitte

  • No publicly disclosed Deloitte audit or advisory engagement with Rush Street Interactive was identified in this search. RSI's confirmed statutory auditor is WithumSmith+Brown, PC, not Deloitte.
