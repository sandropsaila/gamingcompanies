Gentoo Media

Publicly traded iGaming affiliate marketing and media business (Gentoo Media Inc.), spun off from Gaming Innovation Group (GiG) in September 2024, headquartered in Birkirkara, Malta.

Overview

Founded
2007 (as Rebel Penguin); rebranded Gentoo Media September 2024
Operational HQ
Birkirkara, Malta
Group registration
Gentoo Media Inc.; dual listing heritage from Gaming Innovation Group (Oslo: GIG / Nasdaq Stockholm: GIGSEK)
Sector
iGaming affiliate marketing, media & SEO/content services
Employees
Not independently confirmed in this pass
Ownership
Publicly traded; spun off from Gaming Innovation Group's Platform & Sportsbook business (which continued separately as GiG Software PLC) in the September 2024 legal split.

Brands

AskGamblers, Time2Play, CasinoTopsOnline, WSN, Casinomeister

iGaming affiliate & review sites

A portfolio of affiliate/media brands acquired 2023-24 (including Casinomeister.com, acquired June 2024); Titan Inc. provides SEO/content services to online businesses.

Verticals

Affiliate marketing Media / review content SEO & content services (via Titan Inc.)

Markets

Global — English-language iGaming affiliate audiences, primarily serving operators in regulated markets

As an affiliate/media business rather than a licensed gaming operator, Gentoo Media's “markets” are defined by audience geography and the licensed operators it refers traffic to.

MGA Licence(s)

NoteChecked and confirmed not applicable: a search for a Gentoo Media/GiG Media MGA authorisation on the Dynamic Seal returned no matching licensee, consistent with the Gaming Act 2018 not requiring affiliate-marketing businesses to hold an MGA gaming licence in their own right (obligations instead sit with the operators they refer traffic to). Gentoo Media's Malta presence is therefore a corporate/operational base, not a licensing relationship.

Relationship with Malta Affiliate / media — HQ: Malta

  • Gentoo Media is headquartered in Birkirkara, Malta, inheriting Gaming Innovation Group's long-standing Maltese base.
  • As a media/affiliate business, Gentoo Media's relationship with Malta is primarily as an operating-company jurisdiction rather than a gaming-licence jurisdiction.
