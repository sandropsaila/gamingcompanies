Gentoo Media

Publicly traded iGaming affiliate marketing and media company, spun off from Gaming Innovation Group (GiG) in 2024 following its rebrand from GiG Media. Headquartered in Birkirkara, Malta. Gentoo is an affiliate/media business, not a licensed gambling operator.

Relationship with Malta

  • Gentoo Media's own corporate site states its headquarters are in Malta, based at Quad Business Central, Birkirkara.
  • The company traces its roots to Gaming Innovation Group (GiG), long headquartered in Malta, before the 2024 demerger split GiG's B2B platform/sportsbook business from its affiliate/media business (now Gentoo Media).
  • As an affiliate/media business rather than a licensed operator, Gentoo's relationship with Malta is primarily as a base of operations and talent rather than as an MGA gambling licensee.

Relationship with Deloitte

  • No publicly disclosed audit, assurance or advisory engagement between Deloitte and Gentoo Media was identified in this search.
  • As a Malta-headquartered iGaming-adjacent company, Gentoo may fall within the broader population covered by Deloitte Malta's general sector research, but this does not evidence a client relationship.
  • Any actual engagement should be confirmed against Deloitte's internal client and independence records.
