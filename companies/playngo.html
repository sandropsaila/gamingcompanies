Play'n GO

Privately held Swedish slot and casino content studio, licensed and distributed to regulated operators worldwide including via a dedicated Maltese subsidiary, Play'n GO Malta Limited.

Relationship with Malta

  • Play'n GO Malta Limited is a registered entity confirmed on both the MGA's dynamic licensee-verification system and the UK Gambling Commission's public register (which cross-references the Maltese entity for its own licensing purposes).
  • Malta is one of the jurisdictions Play'n GO names directly as part of its licensed distribution footprint, alongside the UK, Gibraltar and Canada.
  • Play'n GO has also received Malta-specific industry recognition, including a 'Best Casino Supplier of the Year' award at Malta's iGaming Excellence Awards and a 2024 Game Studio of the Year award presented in Malta.

Relationship with Deloitte

  • No publicly disclosed audit, assurance or advisory engagement between Deloitte and Play'n GO was identified in this search.
  • As an MGA-licensed B2B content supplier with a Maltese subsidiary, Play'n GO falls within the general population covered by Deloitte Malta's sector-wide iGaming research rather than being evidenced as a client.
  • Any actual engagement should be confirmed against Deloitte's internal client and independence records.
