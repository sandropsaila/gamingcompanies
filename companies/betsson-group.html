Betsson Group

Nasdaq Stockholm-listed operator (Betsson AB) dating to 1963, with operational headquarters in Ta' Xbiex, Malta.

Overview

Founded
1963, Sweden (as AB Restaurang Rouletter — slot machines for restaurants/bars); moved online 2001
Operational HQ
Ta' Xbiex, Malta (“Betsson Experience Centre”)
Group registration
Betsson AB (publ), Nasdaq Stockholm: BETS B
Sector
Online casino, sportsbook, poker & bingo
Employees
≈2,660–2,900 across 17 locations, 70+ nationalities
Ownership
Publicly listed on Nasdaq Stockholm; long-tenured CEO Pontus Lindwall holds a direct 1.46% stake.

Brands

Betsson

Core multi-product brand

Sportsbook, casino, poker and bingo delivered under the Betsson brand plus a portfolio of licensed sub-brands accumulated through acquisitions (e.g. Betsafe, NetPlay TV).

Verticals

Sportsbook Online casino Online poker Online bingo

Markets

25 licensed countries, per company disclosure Nearly 60% of Q4 2024 revenue from locally licensed/taxed jurisdictions

Betsson has committed to exiting or deprioritising unlicensed markets, with the locally-regulated share of revenue continuing to grow.

Key C-suite

Pontus Lindwall
President & Chief Executive Officer

Lindwall has held the CEO role across multiple stints since 2003 (with a brief gap under Ulrik Bengtsson, 2016-17); some 2024 reporting referenced an active CEO search, but subsequent 2024/2025 results continued to list Lindwall as CEO — verify current status directly.

Relationship with Malta Operational HQ: Malta

  • Betsson's operational headquarters, the “Betsson Experience Centre,” is physically located in Ta' Xbiex, Malta, and is referenced directly in the company's own Annual and Sustainability Reports.
  • Betsson is one of the longest-established Nordic-heritage operators based in Malta, alongside Kindred Group.

Relationship with Deloitte

  • A 2019 Betsson corporate governance report references Deloitte having been commissioned for a specific strategic-risk-monitoring audit — a one-off/limited-scope engagement, not confirmation of a statutory audit relationship.
  • No current, publicly disclosed statutory-audit engagement between Deloitte and Betsson was identified; treat any Deloitte connection here as historical and narrow in scope pending direct confirmation.
