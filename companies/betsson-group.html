Betsson Group

Nasdaq Stockholm-listed multi-brand gaming operator dating to 1963. Betsson AB is headquartered in Stockholm, with its operational headquarters in Ta' Xbiex, Malta.

Relationship with Malta

  • Betsson AB's operational headquarters are based in Ta' Xbiex, Malta, even though the listed parent entity is headquartered in Stockholm.
  • Malta's status as a primary EU iGaming jurisdiction is cited by the company as a key reason for basing technology development, customer service, marketing, commercial, finance, legal and compliance functions there.
  • Betsson is a long-standing member of the Malta-based iGaming operator cluster alongside Kindred, LeoVegas and others.
