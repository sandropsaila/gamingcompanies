FILE 17 · OPERATOR PROFILE

ARRISE

B2B iGaming services and technology group — trading as "ARRISE powering Pragmatic Play" — headquartered in Gibraltar with a major operational and licensing presence in Malta.

Overview

Founded
Traces to TopGame Technology (founded 2007), rebranded as Pragmatic Play in 2015 following a 2016 acquisition by IBID Group; ARRISE emerged subsequently as the separate services group (via entities formerly named Pragmatic Services Ltd / Pragmatic Solutions) that operationally powers the Pragmatic Play brand
Operational HQ
Gibraltar, with major offices in Malta (Sliema), Romania, Bulgaria, Serbia, Ukraine, Latvia, India, Canada, Poland and the UAE
Group registration
No single public parent; operates through a network of jurisdiction-specific entities (e.g. Arrise Services Limited in the UK, Arrise Solutions (Malta) Limited, Arrise Solutions (India) Private Limited), commercially and operationally tied to the privately held Pragmatic Play group
Sector
B2B iGaming content and services: slot and live casino game development, studio operations, technology/IT, HR, marketing and regulatory compliance support
Employees
Reported at 5,000–10,000 globally (third-party estimate); Pragmatic Play's own directly employed headcount is cited separately at ≈1,200
Ownership
Privately held; commercially tied to Pragmatic Play, which is owned by a private investor group led by Veridian (Gibraltar) Limited. No shareholder data is publicly disclosed for either entity. Historical lineage includes IBID Group and TopGame co-founder David Barzilay.

Brands

Pragmatic Play

Core B2B content brand ARRISE powers

700+ active slot titles (Big Bass, Sweet Bonanza, Gates of Olympus among its most prominent franchises), releasing 7–8 new games per month; funds the €30m/year "Drops & Wins" prize pool, one of the largest provider-funded promotions in the industry.

ARRISE Live

Live casino operations arm

Handles the operational delivery of Pragmatic Play's live casino studios (roulette, blackjack, baccarat, game shows including Mega Wheel), including a new studio opened in Colombia in October 2025.

Verticals

Online slots (B2B content) Live casino (studio operations) B2B technology & platform services Regulatory compliance support

Pragmatic Play discontinued its sportsbook, virtual sports and bingo verticals following a 2026 strategic review, refocusing solely on slots and live casino.

Markets

30+ regulated markets globally United Kingdom Malta Italy, Sweden Ontario, Canada Brazil, Colombia

Content is distributed to over 2,000 licensed operator partners — including bet365, Betsson, Entain, Flutter Entertainment and William Hill — via a single API integration across 30+ languages and 170+ currencies.

Stock Exchange Listings

Privately held; no stock exchange listing and no SEC registration. Trade press has speculated about a possible future IPO given the market valuations achieved by comparable listed suppliers (e.g. Evolution AB), but the owners have never publicly indicated any listing plans.

Relationship with Malta MGA licensed — commercial & compliance hub

  • Arrise Solutions (Malta) Limited holds a live B2B Critical Supply licence (MGA/B2B/317/2016), confirmed directly via the MGA's Dynamic Seal, registered at Sliema.
  • Malta is described in company and third-party materials as a commercial and compliance hub for the group, alongside its Gibraltar legal/corporate leadership base and India/Bulgaria/Romania development and production centres.
  • A 2024 investigative piece flagged notable intercompany financial flows between the group's Malta and UK entities (see Negative Media) — worth noting for a Maltese due-diligence file, though it stopped short of alleging non-compliance.

Relationship with Deloitte

  • No publicly disclosed Deloitte audit or advisory engagement with ARRISE or Pragmatic Play was identified in this search.
