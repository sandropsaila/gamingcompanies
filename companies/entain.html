Entain

FTSE 100 multi-brand sports betting and gaming group (Ladbrokes, Coral, bwin, partypoker and others), formerly GVC Holdings. Headquartered in the Isle of Man, with a large Malta-based operational footprint across its brand portfolio.

Relationship with Malta

  • Entain operates a significant Malta presence supporting several of its brands (including bwin and international sportsbook operations), consistent with Malta's role as a major EU hub for the group's regulated online business.
  • Malta sits alongside the Isle of Man (group HQ) and other jurisdictions as part of Entain's multi-entity regulatory structure.
  • Individual Entain-Malta entities and their specific MGA licences were not separately itemised in this search — see the MGA Licensee Register for entity-level detail.
