FILE 08 · OPERATOR PROFILE

Entain

FTSE 100 owner of Ladbrokes, Coral, bwin and partypoker (Entain plc, LSE: ENT), headquartered in the UK with a large Malta operational base.

Overview

Founded
2004 (as Gaming VC Holdings); rebranded GVC Holdings, then Entain plc in December 2020
Operational HQ
London, UK (tax resident); large operational presence in Malta
Group registration
Entain plc, LSE: ENT, FTSE 100 constituent
Sector
Sports betting & gaming — online and retail
Employees
Large multi-country workforce; see Entain's Annual Report 2024 for the current headcount
Ownership
Publicly listed (FTSE 100); no single controlling shareholder identified in this search.

Brands

Ladbrokes / Coral

UK retail & online

Acquired via the £4bn Ladbrokes Coral Group acquisition in 2018 — at the time the largest deal in the sector's history.

bwin / partypoker / Sportingbet

International online brands

Multi-market online sportsbook, poker and casino brands.

BetMGM

US joint venture (50%)

Joint venture with MGM Resorts; BetMGM guided to $2.4–2.5bn FY25 revenue with positive EBITDA.

Verticals

Sportsbook Online casino Online poker Online bingo UK & European retail betting shops

Markets

United Kingdom Italy Belgium Netherlands Croatia Poland Georgia Latvia Australia United States (via BetMGM)

Entain describes itself as the only global operator exclusively serving domestically regulated or regulating markets, across 30+ territories.

Relationship with Malta Malta footprint
