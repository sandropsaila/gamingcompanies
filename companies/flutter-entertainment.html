FILE 16 · OPERATOR PROFILE

Flutter Entertainment

The world's largest online sports betting and iGaming group by revenue — owner of FanDuel, PokerStars, Paddy Power, Betfair, Sky Betting & Gaming, Sportsbet and Sisal — registered in Dublin with its operational headquarters in New York.

Overview

Founded
Lineage to 1988 (Paddy Power, Ireland); Paddy Power merged with Betfair in 2016 to form Paddy Power Betfair plc, renamed Flutter Entertainment plc on 28 May 2019
Operational HQ
New York, USA (operational); registered office Belfield Office Park, Clonskeagh, Dublin 4, Ireland
Group registration
Flutter Entertainment plc, incorporated in Ireland; primary listing NYSE: FLUT (since 29 January 2024); previously also LSE-listed, with a Standard Listing the company announced it will exit in 2026 to retain a NYSE-only presence
Sector
Sportsbook, iGaming (casino/poker/bingo), exchange betting, daily fantasy sports, lottery and pari-mutuel wagering
Employees
Not independently confirmed in this pass; a large multi-country workforce across its divisions
Ownership
Publicly listed; no single controlling shareholder. Fox Corporation holds an option (from the 2019 Stars Group acquisition) to acquire an 18.6% stake in FanDuel; Boyd Gaming sold its remaining 5% FanDuel stake to Flutter in 2025 for ≈$1.7bn, giving Flutter full ownership of FanDuel.

Brands

FanDuel

US sportsbook, iGaming & DFS — Flutter's largest brand

US online sportsbook market leader (≈44% GGR share, 25% iGaming share as of Dec 2024, per Flutter's own 10-K); also offers daily fantasy sports and racing/TV wagering. Constituted 41% of group revenue in FY2024.

PokerStars

Global online poker — International division

World's largest online poker room, operated in Malta by TSG Interactive Gaming Europe Limited; acquired via Flutter's 2020 purchase of The Stars Group.

Paddy Power & Betfair

UK & Ireland sportsbook, exchange and casino

Legacy brands from the 2016 Paddy Power/Betfair merger; Betfair also operates a peer-to-peer betting exchange internationally through Betfair International PLC, licensed in Malta.

Sky Betting & Gaming, tombola

UK & Ireland sportsbook and bingo

UK-focused online sportsbook and iGaming (Sky Betting & Gaming) and online bingo (tombola) brands within the UKI division, which also runs 563 Paddy Power betting shops.

Sportsbet

Australia sportsbook

Leading Australian online sports betting brand; International division also includes Sisal (Italy's largest iGaming operator by revenue, acquired 2022), Adjarabet (Georgia), Junglee Games (India rummy) and MaxBet.

Verticals

Sportsbook / fixed-odds betting Online casino Online poker Online bingo Betting exchange (peer-to-peer) Daily fantasy sports Lottery (via Sisal) Racing / pari-mutuel wagering

Markets

United States (FanDuel, live sports betting/iGaming states) United Kingdom & Ireland Australia (Sportsbet) Italy (Sisal) Georgia (Adjarabet) India (Junglee Games) Brazil (entering, via PokerStars/local brand presence) 100+ countries overall

Flutter organises reporting into three divisions: US (FanDuel), UK & Ireland (Sky Betting & Gaming, Paddy Power, Betfair, tombola), and International (Sisal, PokerStars, Betfair International, Adjarabet, Junglee Games, MaxBet, Sportsbet).

Relationship with Malta MGA licensed — operational office

  • Two major Flutter brands are licensed and operated out of Malta: PokerStars (via TSG Interactive Gaming Europe Limited, MGA/B2C/213/2011) and Betfair International (MGA/CRP/131/2006-02) — both registered at Spinola Park, St Julian's, an address the MGA's own Dynamic Seal explicitly labels as belonging to "The Flutter Group."
  • This makes Malta one of Flutter's operational hubs for its International division brands, distinct from its Dublin registered office and New York operational headquarters.

Relationship with Deloitte

  • No publicly disclosed Deloitte audit or advisory engagement with Flutter Entertainment was identified in this search. Flutter's confirmed statutory auditor is KPMG (Ireland), not Deloitte.
