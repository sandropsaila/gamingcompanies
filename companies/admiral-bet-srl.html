FILE 18 · OPERATOR PROFILE

Admiral Bet Srl

Italian ADM-licensed online betting and casino concessionaire (brand AdmiralBet), part of the NOVOMATIC group and under the direction and coordination of Greentube Malta Limited.

Overview

Legal entity
Admiral Bet S.r.l. (single-member company), registered office Via Benedetto Croce 122-124, 00142 Rome. Italian Registro Imprese (Lazio) no. 04236330405; share capital €11,000 medium confidence
Entity identification caveat
The registry number above appears on 2023 documents under the name Admiral Sport S.r.l., while ADM-concession listings name Admiral Bet S.r.l. as holder of the AdmiralBet concession. NOVOMATIC's own timeline records Admiral Bet Srl as acquired in 2010. That the two names are the same legal person (a renaming) is an inference from the shared brand, website and address — confirm with a current Visura camerale before relying on it.
Parent / control
Corporate documents state the company is "subject to direction and coordination" by Greentube Malta Ltd (Malta reg. C 38281). Ultimate parent: NOVOMATIC AG (Gumpoldskirchen, Austria; FN 69548b), with the Italian holding NOVOMATIC Italia S.p.A. in Rome.
Regulator
Agenzia delle Dogane e dei Monopoli (ADM), remote gaming (GAD) concession. Not an MGA licensee in its own right (see Markets).
Group scale in Italy
NOVOMATIC Italia reports 4,200+ staff and about 300 directly managed gaming halls in Italy (company site, 2026).
Ownership
Privately held group (founded 1980 by Johann F. Graf). No separate shareholder data for Admiral Bet Srl was found beyond the sole-member structure.

Brands

AdmiralBet (admiralbet.it)

Flagship Italian online brand of the NOVOMATIC group

Online sports betting and casino, positioned as the public face of the Admiral retail network. Reported ADM concession no. 16048 (see conflict note under Markets).

Sister Italian remote concessions (separate entities)

Not part of Admiral Bet Srl — shown for context

HBG Online Gaming S.r.l. (QuiGioco, concession 16015, acquired by NOVOMATIC Italia in 2022) and Greentube Malta's own Italian-facing concession are reported as the group's other remote concessions. Admiral-branded retail (Admiral Club halls) is operated by other group companies.

Markets

Italy

Italy only, with registration requiring an Italian tax code. Customers are tied to the ADM Registro Unico degli Autoesclusi (RUA).

MGA Authorisation

N/A — not an MGA licensee (checked 8 Oct 2026). Admiral Bet Srl operates under an Italian ADM concession, not an MGA licence, and no MGA authorisation in its name was found. Its parent entity Greentube Malta Limited is listed by the MGA as MGA/CRP/120/2006 (B2B) in an MGA seal page index; Greentube Malta's B2C licence MGA/B2C/823/2020 is recorded in File 12 (Greentube). Live-check caveat: the seal link used in File 12 returned a different licensee name when fetched today, so the Greentube seal needs re-verifying before it is quoted as confirmed.

Relationship with Malta Maltese parent (direction & coordination)

  • Admiral Bet's own documents state it is subject to direction and coordination by Greentube Malta Ltd (Maltese company no. C 38281; VAT MT20638823). This is the main Malta link.
  • Greentube Malta Limited holds MGA licences per File 12 (B2B MGA/CRP/120/2006; B2C MGA/B2C/823/2020), subject to re-verification as noted above.
  • Admiral Bet Srl itself has no MGA authorisation; its regulator is Italy's ADM.
  • Group Malta presence is wider than Greentube: NOVOMATIC's 2023 consolidation scope added Maltese entities S&S Capitals Limited and Alteatec IT Solutions Limited.

Relationship with Deloitte

  • No publicly disclosed Deloitte audit or advisory engagement with Admiral Bet Srl, NOVOMATIC Italia or NOVOMATIC AG was identified in this search.
  • This is an absence of evidence only: the group auditor was not identified (see Auditors), so a Deloitte network audit role cannot be ruled out.
