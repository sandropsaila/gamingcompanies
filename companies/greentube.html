Greentube

NOVOMATIC's Digital Gaming and Entertainment division, headquartered in Vienna, Austria, with a Maltese subsidiary (Greentube Malta Limited) operating since 2006.

Overview

Founded
1998; became Greentube Internet Entertainment Solutions AG in 2000; joined the NOVOMATIC Group in 2010
Operational HQ
Vienna, Austria (Greentube GmbH)
Group registration
Greentube GmbH, Vienna (parent: NOVOMATIC AG); Maltese subsidiary Greentube Malta Limited
Sector
B2B/B2C online casino content & platform supplier
Employees
≈570 (per third-party estimate)
Ownership
Wholly owned within the NOVOMATIC Group, itself controlled by two Austrian private foundations (JFG Privatstiftung, holding 75.1% of Novo Invest GmbH, and VZZZ Privatstiftung).

Brands

Greentube

Core B2B/B2C content & platform brand

Supplies casino content and platform services as NOVOMATIC's digital arm; also operates GREENTUBE BETTING MALTA LTD (established May 2023, wholly owned by Greentube GmbH).

Stock Exchange Listings

Greentube itself is not listed; it is a wholly owned division of privately held NOVOMATIC AG (ultimately controlled by Austrian foundations JFG Privatstiftung and VZZZ Privatstiftung). A separate NOVOMATIC-controlled company, Ainsworth Game Technology, is listed on the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX: AGI) — but this is a distinct entity from Greentube and not itself SEC-registered.

Relationship with Malta Malta subsidiary since 2006

  • Greentube Malta Limited has operated in Malta since 2006, with an additional Maltese betting entity (Greentube Betting Malta Ltd) established in 2023.
  • Public reporting has scrutinised the scale of intra-group revenue-share payments from Greentube Malta to its Austrian parent relative to Malta's tax-refund system — a governance/tax-structuring point worth noting for due-diligence purposes, not a confirmed finding of wrongdoing.
