Relationship with Malta

  • Greentube Malta has operated as part of the Greentube group since 2006, described by the company as a frontrunner within Malta's online gaming sector.
  • The Malta entity operates across both B2C and B2B activities, contributing to the group's continued growth and product innovation.
  • Greentube holds licences from the UK Gambling Commission and the Malta Gaming Authority, positioning Malta as one of its core regulated-market bases alongside its Austrian headquarters.
